Kobold

Divided we stand · levels at 2 / 4 different pieces

A 'pair' means identical pieces (same piece and same star) on your board. Kobolds don't auto-combine on the bench.

  1. 2

    At battle start, each group of identical pieces on your board with an even count summons one extra copy of that piece for the battle. The copy has no items. This works for any piece, not just Kobolds.

    Note: The tooltip says “each two paired” pieces. Each group of identical pieces with an even count summons one copy, so 4 identical pieces still give only one copy and 3 give none.

  2. 4

    Each unpaired Kobold copies a random unpaired non-Kobold ally of the same star. The Kobold turns into that piece (frozen in wax for 4-6s, expires after 15-20s), and an extra copy of that ally is also summoned. Each ally can only be copied once.

    Note: The tooltip doesn't say that the reshaped Kobold is frozen for 4-6 seconds and expires after 15-20 seconds.

Official in-game tooltip

SPECIES TRAIT: Divided we stand
Active when you have at least (N) different Kobold pieces on board. (Multiple effects stack)

(2) Kobolds: Each two paired ally pieces on board will summon a replica when battle starts.
(4) Kobolds: Every unpaired Kobold piece will reshape itself into a non-Kobold ally of the same rank before battle starts.

Aggressive, xenophobic, yet industrious small humanoid creatures living in tunnels and caves deep below the surface. Known for not particularly intelligent, notoriously cowardly and individually weak, the dimunitive beings possess great potential in group combats. A candle on top of the head distinguishes the Kobolds.
