Race

Tauren

Totem of the Clan · levels at 2 / 4 different pieces

Totems are spawned once per team (not once per Tauren), at the start of battle. Totems take only 1 damage per melee hit and 2 per ranged hit.

  1. 2

    Summons a Scarecrow Totem that taunts enemies. Its HP is 2.5 x the number of enemy pieces (counting up to 8, so max 20).

    Note: The tooltip calls it a Taunt Totem; in game it is the Scarecrow Totem. Its HP counts at most 8 enemy pieces.

  2. 4

    Also summons a Woodpecker Totem (29% chance when an enemy casts: silence 3s + 200 magic damage) and a Horn Totem (10% chance when an enemy attacks: disarm 2s + 100 physical damage). Their HP equals the number of enemy pieces (max 8).

    Note: The tooltip says the Horn Totem disarms for 3 seconds and deals 250 damage. The game uses 2 seconds and 100 damage.
