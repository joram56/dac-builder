Evasion · levels at 3 / 6 / 9 different pieces

  1. 3

    Your Elves gain +25% evasion.

  2. 6

    Your Elves gain another +40% evasion, about 55% in total with the (3) bonus.

  3. 9

    When an Elf dodges an attack, it turns invisible for 1s and summons an illusion of the attacker on your side for 7s. The illusion deals 50% damage and has no mana. 5s cooldown per Elf.

    Note: The tooltip describes a single mirrored attack. The game actually summons a 7-second illusion of the attacker, and each Elf can only do this every 5 seconds.
