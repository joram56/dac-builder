Human

Spark of Mind · levels at 3 / 6 different pieces

Humans give courier XP rather than combat stats. XP from Humans can take you past the normal level cap.

  1. 3

    Gain 2 courier XP at the start of every round.

  2. 6

    If you win a battle on your own board (creep rounds count) with at least one Human still alive, gain (courier level - 2) XP at the start of the next round.

    Note: The tooltip doesn't say that at least one Human must survive the win. Creep round wins also count.
