Warrior

Fortified Armor · levels at 3 / 6 / 9 different pieces

Every Warrior piece: Below 50% HP, Warriors get double the armor from Fortified Armor.

  1. 3

    Warriors get +3 armor, doubled to +6 below 50% HP.

  2. 6

    Warriors get another +12 armor: +15 in total, or +30 below 50% HP.

  3. 9

    All allies reflect pure damage equal to 1.5x their current armor every time they take physical or magical damage (3x below 50% HP).

    Note: The tooltip suggests only Warriors reflect. All allies do, and the reflect doubles to 3× armor below 50% HP.
