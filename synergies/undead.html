Undead

Plague · levels at 3 / 6 / 9 different pieces

Hits every non-Undead piece on both sides, including your own, except pieces holding Ascetic's Cap. Only base HP is reduced.

  1. 3

    All non-Undead pieces on the board lose 10% max HP.

  2. 6

    They lose another 30% (-40% total).

  3. 9

    Plagued pieces also lose 5% max HP every 2s, down to a minimum of 5% of their base max HP.

    Note: The tooltip says every 2.5 seconds; the game uses every 2 seconds.
