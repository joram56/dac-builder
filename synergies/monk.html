Drunken Stagger · levels at 2 / 4 / 6 different pieces

Proc chances grow with the damage dealt and can be raised by luck effects. (2) only works on targets below 20% HP. (4) only works on targets at 40% HP or higher.

  1. 2

    Melee Monks have a chance to instantly kill an enemy below 20% HP when they damage it. The chance is higher for bigger hits and lower target HP (roughly 10-23% for a 150-damage hit at 15%-5% HP).

  2. 4

    All Monks have a chance to halve the current HP of an enemy at 40%+ HP when they damage it. Chance = 20% x √(damage/100) (for example 20% for 100 damage, 40% for 400).

  3. 6

    Monks heal for the HP removed by their halving effect.

    Note: The heal from an instant kill is calculated after the target dies, so in practice only the halving effect heals.
