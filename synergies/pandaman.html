Synergies / Pandaren

Race

Pandaren

Hospitality · levels at 1 / 2 different pieces

Rolls happen at the start of every round. Caught pieces go to your bench and are removed from the shared pool. A Lucky Pendant on any of your pieces raises the chance.

  1. 1

    Each round, a chance to receive a random piece that shares a race with one of your board pieces. Pieces already on your board and pieces you already have at 3 stars are skipped. The chance starts at ~6.8%, rises after every miss and resets when you catch something.

    Note: The tooltip gives no numbers. The base chance is about 7%, and it grows each round you miss.

  2. 2

    Also, each Pandaren on your board has a 15% chance (same pity and luck multipliers) to bring a copy of itself.

    Note: The tooltip gives no numbers. Each Pandaren has a 15% base chance, with the same miss bonus.

Official in-game tooltip

SPECIES TRAIT: Hospitality
Active when you have at least (N) different pieces of this species on board. (Multiple effects stack)

(1) Pandaren: Have a chance to invite a random un-emerged chess piece of the same species with a chess piece on the board from the chess piece pool to join the team at the beginning of the round.
(2) Pandaren: Each Pandaren chess piece on board has a chance to invite a piece of the same name with it.

Ten thousand years ago the last emperor of the Pandaren Empire surrounded his entire territory in the misty protection in order to save his people from the Great Havoc. Now the ancient species has once again revealed themselves and they are literally a mist to the others.
