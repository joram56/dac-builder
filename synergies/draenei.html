Race

Draenei

Blessing of the Naaru · levels at 2 / 4 different pieces

  1. 2

    Every 4s, each Draenei hits the nearest enemy for 50 + 2% of that enemy's max HP as pure damage and heals itself for the same amount.

  2. 4

    All allies store the healing they receive. Once 100 or more is stored, it is released as pure damage (up to 300) to every enemy within 300 range. 5s cooldown.

    Note: The tooltip says the damage is split among nearby enemies. In fact every enemy within range takes the full amount (max 300, 5-second cooldown).
