Hunter

Aimed Shot · levels at 3 / 6 / 9 different pieces

Every Hunter piece: Hunters always attack the enemy with the lowest current health within attack range. Wizards and other low-priority units are picked last.

Debuffs from (3) and (6) are separate and stack. When both bonuses are active, one precise shot applies both (-13 armor).

  1. 3

    Hunters' attacks have a 50% chance to be a precise shot: it can't miss and gives -5 armor for 5s.

  2. 6

    Hunters get a separate 80% chance for a precise shot: it can't miss and gives -8 armor for 8s. This stacks with the (3) debuff.

  3. 9

    Precise shots also knock the target back 1 grid and trigger an extra attack (at most once every 0.5s).

    Note: One in-game buff description says 2 grids of knockback; the game uses 1.
