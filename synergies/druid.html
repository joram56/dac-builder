Synergies / Druid

Class

Druid

Synergy · levels at 2 / 4 / 6 different pieces

Every Druid piece: When you make a ★★★ Druid, the remaining copies of that Druid are not removed from the shared pool.

Only different Druids on the board count. The 3-Wizard “unlock all levels” effect doesn't help Druid combining, because combining happens during preparation.

  1. 2

    Two identical Druid★ combine into a Druid★★. An Io★ plus one Druid★ also combine.

  2. 4

    Two identical Druid★★ combine into a Druid★★★. An Io★★ plus one Druid★★ also combine.

  3. 6

    Two identical Druid★★★ combine into the Altar of Spirits. It gets Altar Power stacks equal to the Druid★★★'s level (5-8) and removes all Druids from the shared pool. Each stack means 10% less damage taken and 10% more damage dealt, multiplying (8 stacks: 43% damage taken, 214% damage dealt). At battle start the Altar shares its stacks with nearby friendly Druids it tethers.

    Note: The tooltip suggests the Altar Power stacks add up; they multiply.
