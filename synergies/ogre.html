Race

Ogre

Iron Fist · levels at 2 different pieces

  1. 2

    At battle start, each Ogre eats every adjacent non-Ogre ally (up, down, left, right) of equal or lower level. It gains 60% of their current HP as max HP and 60% of their attack damage. If it survives a won battle, the eaten pieces' levels are added to its own when working out damage to the losing player.

    Note: The tooltip says allies of lower level. Ogres also eat allies of equal level. The extra damage to the losing player is smaller than the tooltip suggests.

Official in-game tooltip

SPECIES TRAIT: Iron Fist
Active when you have at least (N) different pieces of this species on board. (Multiple effects stack)

(2) Ogres: Each ogre can eat up allies nearby (A cross of 4 grids) whose level is lower than itself and gains their 60% HP and Damage. Deals extra damage to enemys courier equals to the sum of all its victims when winning an away-home battle.
