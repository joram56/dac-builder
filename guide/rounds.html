Rounds & combat

Every round has a preparation phase, where you buy and arrange pieces, followed by a battle.

Timers

  • Preparation: 35 seconds in round 1, 25 seconds in rounds 2–11, and 35 seconds from round 12.
  • Battle: up to 50 seconds. If time runs out, the battle is a draw.

Fighting other players

In player rounds you fight a home battle on your own board against a copy of an opponent's line-up. Synergies are worked out at the start of each battle from the pieces on the board.

If you lose, your courier takes damage for every enemy piece still alive. Each piece deals 1 + (level ÷ 3), rounded down:

  • Zeus adds 10% / 15% / 25% (by star) of your current HP on top.
  • Temporary summons count at half level. Items like Witless Shako and Ogre Hood raise their holder's level.
  • Some talents and relics change the damage, for example Guardian (−20%).
  • A draw deals no damage.

Creep rounds

Rounds 1, 2, 3 and every 5th round from 10 to 50 are fought against neutral creeps. Losing a creep round costs no HP.

Loot from creep rounds

  • Creeps drop gold when they die. Stronger creeps (level 3 and up) can also drop an item; the higher the creep's level, the better the odds and tier. After 5 such creeps in a row drop nothing, the next one always drops a tier-1 item.

A loot box (Spoil Token) lets you pick 1 of 3 random items of that tier. A relic box lets you pick 1 of 3 relics. See Items, relics & talents.

Elimination and the Grand Final

  • When a player is knocked out, part of their gold is scattered among the surviving players, and some of their items may drop to survivors too. The player who dealt the final blow gets bonus gold.
  • When only two players are left, the Grand Final begins. After a few rounds fatigue sets in, halving both couriers' HP every round until one of them falls.
